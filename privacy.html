ZRØ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At ZRØ, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

ZRØ is a premium non-alcoholic beverage concept backed by Zaikajaroori Food & Beverage Pvt. Ltd.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • City or location
  • Enquiry details
  • Event registration information
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide through our forms

We do not ask you to provide personal information unless it is required for a specific enquiry, registration, partnership, distribution, or other interaction with us.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Contact you regarding your request
  • Process event registrations
  • Handle distribution, retail, HORECA, partnership or business enquiries
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Communicate important information related to our services or enquiries

We will use your information only for legitimate business and communication purposes.

3. Information Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information submitted through our website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and user experience.

Cookies may help us understand how visitors use our website and improve website performance.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as hosting providers, analytics tools, maps, social media platforms or other technology services.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

ZRØ is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services that may be linked from our website.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or social media platforms.

Once you leave the ZRØ website, the privacy practices of the external website will apply. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies before providing personal information.

7. Data Retention

We may retain enquiry and communication information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, maintain business records, provide services, or meet applicable legal and business requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, removed.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us if you want to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Ask about the use of your information
  • Request deletion of information where applicable
  • Withdraw a previous enquiry or communication request

Requests may be subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technology or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the ZRØ team.
